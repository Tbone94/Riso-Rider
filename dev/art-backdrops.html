Side backdrops: readability

Every level's side backdrop printed as the app does, before (the frozen pre-round-B riso.js: calm .55, pale .5, dots everywhere) and after (Riso.printSideBackdrop, which follows Riso.SCENES[scene].side). The overlay is the real side.js showing solutions[0] and its ghost ride. Band = backdrop pixels within 18 design units of a block edge, the goal ring or the solution's ride path. Density = colour distance from bare paper (CIE76 ΔE / 100, on 4×4-averaged pixels so grain and dots blend as the eye blends them), i.e. how much ink the eye sees there, loud light inks included. Lower band density means gameplay reads more clearly; rest is the rest of the sheet, which should keep its richness. p90 is the 90th-percentile band density (dark patches behind gameplay). Dark is the plain lightness loss (L*) in the band, since the game's key ink reads by value.
