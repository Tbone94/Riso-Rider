# Line-rider-style game — brainstorm

Goal: something that plays like Line Rider (you draw, a rider rides it) but is unique in how it plays and really pretty.

## How it could play differently

**What you draw**
- **Draw wind, not ground.** The rider is a paper crane or a leaf; your strokes are currents it rides. Floatier, more graceful.
- **Draw gravity.** Paint gravity wells; the rider is a comet slingshotting between them. Paths are orbits, so they're beautiful by default.
- **Draw with light.** The rider only exists where light hits. You draw beams; shadows are holes in the track.
- **Your line is a rope.** Strokes sag, stretch and twang under the rider's weight, somewhere between a bridge and a trampoline.

**When you draw**
- **Draw live while it rides.** The rider never stops; you draw just ahead of it as the camera scrolls. Your line fades a few seconds after the rider passes. Rhythm/flow game.
- **Ink is limited.** Collect things mid-ride to get more ink, so you plan short, clever paths instead of big safety nets.

**What counts as success**
- **The track is a song.** Every segment the rider touches plays a note: pitch from height, rhythm from spacing. You compose music you can then watch.
- **The trail is a painting.** The rider leaves paint; the goal is a trail that traces a shape or looks beautiful, not just reaching the finish.
- **Constellations.** Lines can only connect fixed stars, so each level is a physics puzzle made of a few precise connections.

**Surface materials** (work with any of the above): ice, rubber, sticky honey, boost lines, lines that crumble after one use.

## How it could look

- **Sumi-e ink wash:** strokes thicken with speed, cream paper grain, ink bleeds slightly, a red seal stamp when you finish.
- **Watercolor bloom:** the line is pencil-thin until the rider touches it, then color blooms outward into the paper.
- **Night sky / aurora:** lines are ribbons of light on deep blue, the rider trails stardust, finished tracks become constellations.
- **Paper cutout diorama:** layered paper with parallax depth; lines are strips of colored paper with soft drop shadows.
- **Risograph print:** 2–3 ink colors (pink, teal, yellow), grain, slight misregistration. Looks like a zine.
- **Embroidery:** the line is stitched thread on fabric, the rider is a needle.
- **Stained glass:** closed loops you draw fill with glowing colored glass.

## Strongest combinations

1. **Needle.** The track is a song + ink wash / watercolor. The rider is a record needle, touching a line plays a note and blooms color. You share tracks as songs.
2. **Afterglow.** Draw live while it rides + night sky. The rider is a spark, you draw light just ahead of it, and it fades behind. Pure flow.
3. **Drift.** Draw wind + sumi-e. A paper crane glides on brush-stroke currents over a misty landscape. Calm and meditative.
4. **Orrery.** Draw gravity + constellations. A comet threads through gravity wells you place; the finished orbits are the art.
